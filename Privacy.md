Privacy Policy — Engine Simulator: iOS

Engine Simulator: iOS does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

The app does not require an account or login and does not use advertising or analytics services.

Engine Simulator: iOS may open external websites, such as the Engine Simulator engine catalog or GitHub, at the user’s request. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies.

Any files imported or used by the app remain on the user’s device unless the user chooses to share them.

For questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact the developer through the Engine Simulator: iOS GitHub repository.
